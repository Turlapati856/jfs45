Nara Chandrababu Naidu (Telugu pronunciation: cbn was born 20 April 1950), commonly known as CBN, is an Indian politician who is currently serving as the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh since 2024. He holds the record of longest-serving Chief Minister in the political history of Telugu states. He is the national president of the Telugu Desam Party (TDP). In 1978, he was elected to the Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly from the Indian National Congress party, and from 1980 to 1982, he served as a minister in the state cabinet. Afterwards, he switched party allegiance and joined TDP, which had been founded by his father-in-law N. T. Rama Rao.[5] Naidu served as a TDP Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) from 1989 to 1995.[6] In 1995, he became the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh.

Details

Birth: Born in Naravaripalle, Chittoor district, Andhra Pradesh, to an agricultural family.

Education: Holds a Bachelor’s and Master’s degree in Economics from Sri Venkateswara University, Tirupati.

Family: Married to Bhuvaneswari, daughter of TDP founder N.T. Rama Rao (NTR). His son, Nara Lokesh, is also a prominent politician and MLA.

Political Career & Leadership

Current Office: in as Chief Minister on June 12, 2024, after leading the TDP-BJP-Janasena alliance to a landslide victory.

Party Leadership: Serving as the National President of the Telugu Desam Party (TDP).

Longest Tenure: He holds the record for being the longest-serving Chief Minister in the history of Telugu states, with previous tenures from 1995–2004 (United AP) and 2014–2019 (Bifurcated AP).

Governance & Vision

"CEO of Andhra Pradesh": Known for his corporate-style governance, he championed liberalization and market reforms in the 1990s, transforming Hyderabad into a global IT hub through projects like HITEC City

Technology & Innovation: A staunch advocate for "Speed of Doing Business," his current 2026 agenda focuses on leveraging AI, Deep Tech, and Digital Public Infrastructure to modernize public service delivery.

Vision 2020: Historically pioneered long-term state planning in collaboration with global consultants to ensure transparent and accountable governance.
